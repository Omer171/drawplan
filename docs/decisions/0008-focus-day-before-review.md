# 0008: On a focus day the focus task wins over a due review

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Decided by: us (our own default; the product owner settled the 14-day review that
  takes the day's slot, but not what happens when it falls on a focus day)

## Decision (Y-statement)

In the context of **the one daily task (R3) now having three possible sources: the
weekly focus skill on Mon, Wed, Fri and Sun (R7), a due review of a mastered skill
(R8), and the normal rotation**, facing **days when a review is due on a focus day,
so one of the two has to wait**, we decided for **the order focus day, then due
review, then rotation. A review that falls on a focus day stays due and is given on
the next non-focus day (at most one day later, since focus days never come two in a
row)**, and neglected **review first (keeps the "every 14 days" promise exactly, but
takes one of the 4 focus days the product owner chose for the neglected skill, so
real neglect would get fixed more slowly), and giving both tasks on the same day
(breaks the one-task-per-day rule, and the product owner chose "takes the slot" over
"extra task")**, to achieve **a neglected skill that keeps all 4 of its focus days,
which matter more because that skill isn't mastered yet, while a mastered skill's
review is delayed by at most one day**, accepting **that reviews sometimes come 15
days apart instead of 14. The next review is counted from the day the review was
actually given, so the delay doesn't add up across reviews.**
